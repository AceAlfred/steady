The plate rule

½ vegetables — salad, cucumber, tomato, broccoli, peppers.

¼–½ protein — chicken, kebab meat, eggs, keso.

a little fat — a thumb of oil, sauce, avocado or cheese.

Use a 23–25 cm plate. No bread, rice, fries or pasta on it.

Why the yo-yo happens

Crash diets lose weight fast, then hunger and habits win and it comes back. This app aims for slow and boring: about 0.5% of your body weight per week, high protein to keep muscle, and daily habits you can keep for years.

Tip of the day

Fasting, the smart way

• Start easy. Begin with 16 h (finish dinner at 20:00, eat lunch at 12:00). Move to 18–24 h only once 16 h feels easy.

• Drink: water, sparkling water, black coffee, tea. Add a pinch of salt if you get a headache.

• Break it gently: protein + veg (eggs, keso, chicken + salad). A fast followed by a kebab-with-fries feast cancels itself out.

• Hit your protein on fast days anyway, in your eating window.

• Training: an easy bike ride or stretching while fasting is fine. Save hard intervals for eating days.

• Stop the fast if you feel dizzy, shaky or unwell. Check with a doctor first if you take medication (especially for diabetes or blood pressure) or have a history of disordered eating.

When you slip

One kebab with bread and fries isn't a failure, it's one meal. Log it, drink water, and make the next meal a plate-rule meal. Never “start again Monday”: start again at the next meal.
